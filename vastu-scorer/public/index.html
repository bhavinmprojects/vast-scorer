Rules, sources and limits

Your plans. Plans you upload and draw are kept only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Scoring depends on where you draw each room, so outline rooms carefully and keep the street at the bottom of the plan.

Windsor example accuracy. Room positions are traced by hand from Lakeview's Windsor brochure (Elevation B, Oct 2025), including every optional layout it shows: library and main-floor bedroom on the ground floor; 4-bath and 5-bedroom on the second. The brochure marks Elevations C and E as similar; their room sizes differ by a few inches but positions match, so elevation does not change the score. Positions are within a few feet. The brochure supplied is the Glenway (Newmarket) version; confirm the Queensville Windsor matches. Whether the library and main-floor bedroom options can be combined is not stated, so they are modelled as alternatives.

Zoning method. Each room is assigned to one of 8 directions by the bearing from the house centre to the room's centre. Rooms whose centre falls within the middle ninth of the footprint count as Brahmasthan. A consultant would use a 16-zone grid and split large rooms across zones.

Kitchen southeast ideal, northwest acceptable, avoid northeast, north and southwest: JK Cement, Casagrand, Morphogenesis; avoid centre: AstrologyVastu.

Master bedroom southwest; south or west acceptable; avoid northeast and southeast: Godrej, HomeBazaar, Nuvo Nirmaan.

Bathrooms avoid northeast and southwest: Godrej. West or northwest preferred is a common but less-sourced convention.

Stairs south, west or southwest; not northeast: RealtyHunting, L&T Realty.

Garage northwest preferred: L&T Realty; southeast or northwest parking: Digit. Southwest is disputed (scored 0).

Living room north, east or northeast: L&T Finance. North and east open, south and west heavy: Udemy course.

Library / study east, west or northeast: Digit.

Entrance east or north most auspicious: PropertyPistol; avoid southwest: TogetherBuying. The door's position along the wall is scored by which half it sits in (the half nearer north/east is preferred). Pada numbering conventions differ between sources, so the exact pada shown is indicative only.

True north. Around Toronto the compass reads roughly 10–13° west of true north. Use the lot skew slider for a street that isn't square to north, and check the site plan's north arrow.

This is a screening tool. Have a Vastu consultant review the chosen lot before signing.
